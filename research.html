Research

18 projects, from string compactifications to flame chemistry. Each record states the question, the methods and where the work was done.

1 Cosmology and theory

Inflation and its embedding in fundamental theory, early-universe relics, and the late-time expansion history.

  • The Kähler stability wall in brane–antibrane inflation

    How much anti-brane tension can a stabilised Kähler volume tolerate before the vacuum that inflation needs disappears?

    2026

  • Inflation under ACT DR6 and DESI DR2

    How strongly do present CMB and large-scale-structure constraints discriminate among non-minimally coupled inflationary scenarios?

    March – July 2025 · Institute of Theoretical Physics, University of Warsaw

  • Primordial black holes and induced gravitational waves in a bounce

    Can loop-corrected curvature spectra, non-Gaussian collapse and pulsar-timing signals be reconciled within a single non-singular history?

    June – December 2024 · Centre for Cosmology and Science Popularization, SGT University

  • Quantum-deformed phase-space geometry and emergent inflation

    What effective four-dimensional dynamics emerge when gravity is formulated on a quantum-deformed phase-space geometry?

    September 2024 – March 2026 · Future University in Egypt and Egyptian Center for Theoretical Physics

  • Emergent cosmology in causal dissipative f(T) gravity

    Can causal dissipation in torsion-based gravity support a non-singular emergent branch without an explicit cosmological constant?

    March – May 2025 · BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus

  • An effective running Hubble constant from binned Pantheon data

    Can redshift-binned supernova data distinguish a genuinely evolving expansion scale from the imprint of the background model?

    March – September 2026 · National Astronomical Observatory of Japan and ENEA–Sapienza University of Rome

  • Conceptual foundations of spacetime geometry

    Which conceptual commitments are hidden inside the geometric language used by relativity and cosmology?

    Minkowski Institute for Fundamental Studies

2 Astrophysics and observation

Transient and time-domain signals used as probes of distances, plasmas and planetary systems.

  • Scintillated binary microlensing of fast radio bursts

    Can interference in a lensed fast radio burst recover the mass geometry of a compact binary and expose a cosmological distance?

    July – September 2025 · Institute of Astronomy, National Tsing Hua University

  • Superluminous supernovae as cosmological probes

    Can Type Ic superluminous supernovae add useful leverage when standard and modified cosmological backgrounds are compared?

    June – October 2023 · Miranda House, University of Delhi

  • Qatar-1b transit-timing analysis

    Do the measured transit times of Qatar-1b contain coherent deviations that point beyond a strictly periodic orbit?

    MIT Exoplanet Research Project

  • Solar radio bursts and coronal plasma diagnostics

    What can the frequency–time drift of solar radio bursts reveal about density, temperature and turbulent energy transport?

    August – October 2022 · Space Advancement and Research Cell

  • Asteroid search and candidate identification

    How can faint moving candidates be separated from static sky structure precisely enough to become credible objects?

    International Astronomical Search Collaboration (NASA partner)

3 Engineering and space systems

Earlier work from my mechanical-engineering training: combustion, heat transfer, structures and space infrastructure.

  • Ammonia–hydrogen combustion and emissions

    How does hydrogen enrichment reshape flame speed, ignition and nitrogen-oxide formation in ammonia combustion?

    July 2023 – January 2024 · CFD Laboratory, BMS College of Engineering

  • Thermal management for high-power battery cells

    Can a compact liquid-and-phase-change architecture keep high-power cells inside a narrow thermal window?

    March – December 2024 · Heat Transfer Laboratory, BMS College of Engineering

  • Bio-inspired aeroacoustics of tubercles and serrations

    Can bio-inspired leading- and trailing-edge geometry reduce airfoil noise without giving up aerodynamic performance?

    July 2023 – July 2025 · Wind Tunnel Laboratory, BMS College of Engineering

  • Power architectures for deep space

    Which power-conversion architectures remain credible under variable solar flux, planetary shadowing and long radiation exposure?

    February 2023 – February 2024 · Deep Space Initiative, Andromeda Research Project

  • Autonomous satellite servicing and hybrid propulsion

    What combination of servicing autonomy, orbital mechanics and hybrid propulsion could make spacecraft infrastructure less disposable?

    July 2022 – July 2024 · Propel Lab 3, BMS College of Engineering

  • Fatigue-resistant lower-limb orthotics

    How can mass, stiffness, stress concentration and fatigue life be balanced in a lower-limb orthotic structure?

    June 2023 – December 2025 · Dassault Systèmes Product Innovation Lab
