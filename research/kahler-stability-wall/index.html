01The question

Why the volume must stay stable

An anti-D3-brane can supply positive inflationary energy. But that same energy can destabilize the compact internal volume that the construction depends on.

The perturbative setup stabilizes the physical Kähler volume, σ = 𝒱2/3, rather than the holomorphic real part alone. The leading nilpotent effective theory becomes a competition between uplift and stabilizing terms.

As anti-D3 tension increases, a local volume minimum approaches the nearby decompactification barrier. At a precise threshold, both stationary points vanish together.

04Scaling and adiabatic breakdown

Critical scaling
near the wall

Near the wall define μ = ac − a > 0. The minimum–barrier separation, Kähler mass squared and barrier height vanish with universal saddle-node powers. The Schur-complement correction has the opposite exponent when inflaton–volume mixing persists.

At the fold, the volume curvature Vχχ tends to zero. Unless the mixing numerator vanishes sufficiently fast, the correction becomes singular and the adiabatic single-field reduction loses control. This does not imply the full multifield theory itself diverges.

06What the result establishes

Scope of
the result

I

Exact local result. The leading nilpotent effective theory has a closed-form positive-tension wall, together with local exponents and an adiabatic-breakdown criterion.

II

Quantitative pre-filter. Warping, tadpole accounting and conifold control make the critical-tension inequality arithmetically restrictive.

III

Remaining global work. Full compactification data, the complete physical mass matrix, residual Coulomb volume dependence and omitted Wilsonian terms must still be checked.
