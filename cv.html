How I approach research

The question comes first.

In cosmology and gravity, a result needs more than an attractive equation. Its assumptions, mathematical consistency and observational implications matter. That is why my work moves between analytical derivations and numerical checks.

My engineering background brings a complementary perspective: modelling is useful when it respects material, thermal, structural or operational constraints. I am interested in problems that benefit from both approaches.
