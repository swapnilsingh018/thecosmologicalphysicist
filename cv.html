ABOUT

Cosmological inverse problems. Nonlinear gravitation. High-fidelity simulation.

I work at the boundary between relativistic astrophysics and advanced numerical modelling, building computational frameworks that connect fundamental physics to quantitative evidence. I am pursuing an MS in Physical and Astrophysical Sciences at the University of Florence, with a background in mechanical engineering.

My research centres on covariance-aware Bayesian inverse problems, nonlinear phase-space dynamics and modified-gravity cosmologies. I develop numerical inference workflows for cosmological observables, interrogate stability in dynamical models and analyse relativistic perturbations through physically constrained computation.

In engineering, I work with coupled thermo-fluid transport, reactive-flow kinetics, finite-element stress analysis and orbital-system dynamics. The common method is rigorous: formulate the governing equations, implement the numerical solver, propagate uncertainty and extract interpretable physical results.
