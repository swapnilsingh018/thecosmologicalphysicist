Research approach

Mathematical clarity.
Computational discipline.

My work approaches questions in cosmology and gravity through analytical models, numerical checks, and a careful reading of observational constraints. Research interests include inflation, primordial black holes, cosmological perturbations, dark energy, and string-inspired effective theories.

My foundation in mechanical engineering also informs studies of space systems, thermofluids, and physical design. Across these areas, I am interested in the point where a model becomes testable—and where its approximations stop being reliable.

01 / Focus

Research directions

  • Early-universe cosmology

    Inflationary dynamics, CMB constraints, primordial fluctuations and their signatures.

  • Gravitation & compact objects

    Black-hole thermodynamics, primordial black holes and associated gravitational-wave phenomenology.

  • Effective theories & stability

    Scalar-field potentials, higher-dimensional models, modulus stabilization and consistency conditions.

  • Scientific computation

    Symbolic derivation, numerical parameter studies, visualization and model comparison.
