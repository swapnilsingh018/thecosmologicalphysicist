About

Theoretical cosmologist and computational physicist, University of Florence.

I am a theoretical physicist working on the early universe. Most of my work concerns inflation: how it can be realised in supergravity and string compactifications, and which models survive the latest observations from ACT, DESI and pulsar timing arrays.

I am completing an MSc in Physical and Astrophysical Sciences at the University of Florence. I came to physics from mechanical engineering and still work that way: alongside analytic calculations I write the numerical solvers, simulations and statistical pipelines that test them.
