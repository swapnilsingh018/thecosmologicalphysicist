ABOUT

Physics, computation & engineering.

I am a physicist and engineer based in Florence, Italy, currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Physical and Astrophysical Sciences at the University of Florence. My academic path began in mechanical engineering and developed toward theoretical cosmology and numerical physics.

My work spans models of the early universe, gravitational dynamics, astrophysical data and computational methods. I combine analytic reasoning with statistical inference and simulation, paying particular attention to physical assumptions, numerical stability and reproducibility.

In parallel, my engineering experience includes computational fluid dynamics, structural analysis, thermal systems and aerospace concepts. I value research that connects mathematical models to measurable or testable physical behaviour.
