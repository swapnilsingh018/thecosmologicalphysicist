About

I am a theoretical physicist working on the early universe. Most of my work concerns inflation: how it can be realised in supergravity and string compactifications, and which models survive the latest observations from ACT, DESI and pulsar timing arrays.

I am completing an MSc in Physical and Astrophysical Sciences at the University of Florence. I came to physics from mechanical engineering and still work that way: alongside analytic calculations I write the numerical solvers, simulations and statistical pipelines that test them.

Latest paper

The Kähler stability wall in perturbative brane–antibrane inflation

S. K. Singh, arXiv:2611.08491 [hep-th], November 2026

Brane–antibrane inflation needs the Kähler volume to stay stabilised while the anti-D3 tension drives inflation. That requirement sets an exact upper bound on the tension. Beyond it, the volume minimum and its barrier merge and disappear.

The paper finds the boundary in closed form, derives how the modulus mass and barrier height vanish as it is approached, and turns the result into necessary conditions on the flux data of candidate compactifications.
