Latest paper

The Kähler Stability Wall in Perturbative Brane–Antibrane Inflation

S. K. Singh, arXiv:2611.08491 [hep-th], November 2026

Brane–antibrane inflation needs the Kähler volume to stay stabilised while the anti-D3 tension drives inflation. That requirement sets an exact upper bound on the tension: past it, the volume minimum and its decompactification barrier merge and disappear.

The paper finds this boundary in closed form, derives how the modulus mass and barrier height scale as it is approached, and turns the result into necessary conditions on the flux data of candidate compactifications.

Research

Three connected questions about where the universe came from and how we can test it.

Inflation and the early universe

Confronting inflationary models with ACT DR6 and DESI DR2, building α-attractor models in supergravity, and following primordial black holes and induced gravitational waves through bouncing cosmologies.

Projects: Inflation under ACT and DESI, Primordial black holes in a bounce

String compactification and quantum gravity

Moduli stabilisation and its limits in brane–antibrane inflation, quantum-deformed phase-space geometry and generalised uncertainty relations, and higher-loop structure in supersymmetric gauge theories.

Projects: The Kähler stability wall, Quantum-deformed phase space

Late-time cosmology and astrophysical probes

Testing whether the Hubble constant runs with redshift in supernova data, emergent cosmologies in dissipative f(T) gravity, and fast-radio-burst lensing as a distance probe.

Projects: A running Hubble constant, Emergent f(T) cosmology, FRB lensing
