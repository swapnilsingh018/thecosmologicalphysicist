Solar radio bursts and coronal plasma diagnostics

What can the frequency–time drift of solar radio bursts reveal about density, temperature and turbulent energy transport?

Dynamic radio spectra, dispersion and plasma gradients used to infer coronal conditions, connecting observation with magnetohydrodynamic models of energy transport.

Methods

  • Radio dispersion
  • Coronal density models
  • MHD energy cascades
