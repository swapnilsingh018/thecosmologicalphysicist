Research / Cosmology and theory

Inflation under ACT DR6 and DESI DR2

How strongly do present CMB and large-scale-structure constraints discriminate among non-minimally coupled inflationary scenarios?

Inflationary observables computed for single-field and non-minimally coupled models, including Jordan–Einstein frame mappings and slow-roll evolution, then scanned against the ACT DR6 and DESI DR2 constraints on the spectral index and tensor-to-scalar ratio.

Methods

  • Jordan–Einstein frame mappings
  • Slow-roll observables
  • Parameter scans against ACT DR6 / DESI DR2
