Research / Cosmology and theory

Primordial black holes and induced gravitational waves in a bounce

Can loop-corrected curvature spectra, non-Gaussian collapse and pulsar-timing signals be reconciled within a single non-singular history?

Primordial-black-hole formation and scalar-induced gravitational waves in non-singular bounce scenarios. The analysis tracks how negative non-Gaussianity changes the black-hole abundance and the induced signal in the pulsar-timing band.

Methods

  • Renormalised curvature spectra
  • Non-Gaussian collapse thresholds
  • Scalar-induced gravitational waves
  • PTA constraints
