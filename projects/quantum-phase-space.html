Research / Cosmology and theory

Quantum-deformed phase-space geometry and emergent inflation

What effective four-dimensional dynamics emerge when gravity is formulated on a quantum-deformed phase-space geometry?

Effective gravitational and inflationary dynamics derived from a deformed Hamiltonian phase space: the induced metric, the equations of motion, and the structure of scalar and tensor perturbations, leading to exact inflationary backgrounds with testable observables.

Methods

  • Hamiltonian phase-space geometry
  • Generalised uncertainty relations
  • Perturbation theory
  • Tensor calculus
