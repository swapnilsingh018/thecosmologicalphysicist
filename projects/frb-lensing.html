Research / Astrophysics and observation

Scintillated binary microlensing of fast radio bursts

Can interference in a lensed fast radio burst recover the mass geometry of a compact binary and expose a cosmological distance?

Gravitational lensing of fast radio bursts by compact binaries, using numerical lens inversion and propagation delays, with scintillation-aware forward models. Alongside, an analysis of high-redshift structure in JWST COSMOS-Web data.

Methods

  • Binary-lens inversion
  • Fermat-potential delays
  • JWST COSMOS-Web
