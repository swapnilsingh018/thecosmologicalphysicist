Privacy

This is the personal research website of Swapnil Kumar Singh, who is responsible for it. It has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no live chat, and it sets no cookies.

Visitor statistics

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It uses no cookies and does not store IP addresses. It records the page visited, the referring site, browser type, screen size and approximate country, and uses a short-lived hash to count each visitor once. The total number of unique visitors is shown at the bottom of the homepage.

The legal basis is a legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregate, how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Other services the site uses

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes connection data such as IP addresses to deliver pages (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers (Google privacy policy).
  • Links to arXiv, INSPIRE-HEP, journal publishers, GitHub and LinkedIn take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Email

If you write to me, I use your message and address only to reply, and keep it no longer than the conversation needs.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of any personal data I hold about you, and you can complain to the Italian data-protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). For any privacy question, write to swapnilsingh.ph@gmail.com.
