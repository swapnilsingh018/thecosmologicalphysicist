The Kähler stability wall

1 The paper in three steps

The boundary

The positive-tension stability wall is found analytically, at the saddle-node point where the Kähler minimum merges with its decompactification barrier.

Where the effective theory breaks

Near the wall the modulus mass and barrier height scale critically. A Schur-complement test shows when integrating out the volume stops being justified.

Flux filters

Throat, tadpole, conifold, normalisation and mass-matrix conditions restrict candidate compact realisations. They are necessary, not sufficient: no compact vacuum is claimed.
